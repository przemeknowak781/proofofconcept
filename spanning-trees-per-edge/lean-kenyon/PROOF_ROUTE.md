# The proof route checked by Lean

This file lists the steps of the proof formalised in this directory, with the names of the Lean
theorems (namespace `Kenyon`; `Act.` means `Kenyon.Act.`). The same route is described in
Section 10 of the paper. Notation: `G` is a finite multigraph with `n` vertices and `m` edges,
`D` its degree matrix, `A` its adjacency matrix (a loop counts twice on the diagonal),
`K(z) = D − zA`, `θ = √(27/8)`, `L = log θ`.

The route keeps the edge inequality of the paper (package `TreeEntropy`) and replaces everything
else. It uses neither the universal cover as an object, nor probability, nor power series.

## Upper bound: `τ(G) < θ^m` for `m ≥ 1`

Class `C'`: every degree is at least 2 and no edge joins two vertices of degree 2 (loops allowed,
connectivity not required).

1. **Dart system with a parameter** (`Levels`). For `G ∈ C'` and `z ∈ [0,1]` the levels
   `h⁰ = 0`, `h^{t+1}_{u→v} = z / (d_u − z Σ_{w≠v} h^t_{w→u})` increase in `t` and in `z`, satisfy
   the a priori bounds and converge to a solution `h(z)` of `h_{u→v} (d_u − z Σ_{w≠v} h_{w→u}) = z`
   (`good_lev`, `lev_le_succ`, `lev_mono_z`, `sol_fix_p`, `sol_fix_m`, `sol_mono_z`).
   With `S_v = Σ_{u→v} h_{u→v}`, `P_v = d_v − z S_v`, `q_e = h_{u→v} h_{v→u}`:
   `Xi z = Σ_v log P_v − Σ_e log (1 − q_e)` and `Wsum z = Σ_v S_v / P_v`.
   At `z = 1` the edge inequality gives `Xi 1 ≤ m L` (`xi_one_le`, `Xi_one_le_edges`).
2. **Comparison of Green functions** (`Green`, `Lap`). If `g ≥ 0` and `K(z) g ≥ 0`, then
   `g(u) ≥ h_{u→v}(z) g(v)` for every dart and `(K(z) g)(v) ≤ P_v(z) g(v)` (`dart_ineq_sol`,
   `green_sol`). For `z < 1`, `K(z)` is positive definite and `K(z) g ≥ 0` implies `g ≥ 0`
   (`Kmat_posDef`, `nonneg_of_Kop_nonneg`). Hence `tr (K(z)⁻¹ A) ≥ Wsum z`
   (`trace_inv_mul_Amat_ge`).
3. **Two one-step inequalities**, for `0 ≤ a ≤ b < 1`.
   * `det N ≤ det M · exp (tr (M⁻¹ N) − n)` for `M` positive definite and `N` positive
     semidefinite (`det_le_det_mul_exp`). With `M = K(a)`, `N = K(b)`:
     `det K(b) ≤ det K(a) · exp (−(b − a) Wsum a)` (`det_Kmat_step`).
   * `Xi b − Xi a ≥ −(b − a) Wsum b` (`Xi_step`). Here `h(b)` is a super-solution of the system
     at `a` (`superSol_sol`), super-solutions form a convex set (`superSol_mix`), and the Bethe
     functional `Bfun a` increases along a segment of super-solutions (`SuperSol.grad_p`,
     `SuperSol.grad_m`, `Bfun_mono`).
4. **Telescoping** (`Bethe`). `F = log det K − Xi` satisfies `F 0 = 0` and
   `F b − F a ≤ (b − a)(Wsum b − Wsum a)` (`logdet_step`). Summing over `N` equal parts of
   `[0, z]` and letting `N → ∞`: `det K(z) ≤ exp (Xi z)` for `0 ≤ z < 1` (`det_Kmat_le_exp_Xi`).
   No derivative of `h(z)` in `z` is used.
5. **From the determinant to trees** (`Forest`, `MatrixTree`). `redMat Es R c z` is
   `diagonal c + z · Laplacian` with the rows and columns of `R` replaced by those of the
   identity; `numForests Es R` counts the sets of `n − |R|` edges of `Es` that join every vertex
   to `R`. For an edge with exactly one end in `R` both satisfy a recursion (`det_redMat_cross`,
   `numForests_le`), hence `z^(n−|R|) · numForests ≤ det` (`numForests_le_det`) and
   `det K(z) ≥ (1 − z) d_r z^(n−1) · numTrees` (`det_Kmat_ge`).
6. **The class** (`Count`, `Amplify`). With `z = 1 − 1/n`: `numTrees ≤ n² e³ θ^m`
   (`Xi_le_one`, `numTrees_le_classC`). For `N + 1` copies glued at a vertex the class is
   preserved and `numTrees^(N+1) ≤ numTrees(glued)` (`two_le_deg_glue`, `no22_glue`,
   `numTrees_pow_le`), so `numTrees ≤ θ^m` (`le_one_of_pow_le`, `numTrees_le_exp_classC`).
7. **All multigraphs** (`Contract`, `Main`). Induction on `m` (`numTrees_le_pow`):
   `n ≤ 1` (`numTrees_le_one`); a vertex not joined to another vertex gives `τ = 0`
   (`exists_edge_of_conn`); a vertex of degree 1 with edge `f` gives `τ(G) ≤ τ(G/f)`
   (`pendant_step`, `numTreesWith_le_contract`); the class; otherwise an edge `e`, not a loop,
   joins two vertices of degree 2 and `τ(G) ≤ τ(G/e) + τ(G − e) ≤ θ^(m−1) + θ^(m−2) ≤ θ^m`
   (`numTrees_split`, `numTreesWithout_le_delete`, `deg_delete`, `cK_add_one_le`).
   Strictness by parity (`kenyon_bound`, `kenyon_bound_real`).

## Sharpness: for every `c < 27/8` a multigraph with `τ² > c^m`

8. **The graphs** (`Sanov`). `HS n` is the group of permutations of `(ℤ/n)²` generated by
   `(x,y) ↦ (x+2y, y)` and `(x,y) ↦ (x, y+2x)`; `cayAct n` is its action on itself by right
   multiplication; the graph has the vertices `HS n` and the edges `HS n × Fin 2`. It is
   connected (`cay_conn`). If `3^J + 1 < n`, no nonempty reduced word of length at most `J`
   fixes a vertex (`cay_free`); this is Sanov's argument on integer vectors (`inv_step`,
   `inv_ptZ`, `bd_ptZ`, `sanov`).
9. **Regular graphs** (`Regular`). On a 4-regular multigraph the levels are constant, their
   limit `sVal z` satisfies `sVal z (4 − 3 z sVal z) = z`, `sVal z ≤ z/3`, `sVal 1 = 1/3`
   (`lev_const`, `sVal_fix`, `sVal_le`, `sVal_one`), `Xi 1 = n log (27/8)` (`Xi_one_reg`) and
   `Xi b − Xi a ≤ −(b − a) Wsum a` (`Xi_step_reg`).
10. **Upper bound for the Green function** (`Cayley`). `uW v j x` is the number of reduced words
    of length `j` from `v` to `x`, defined by a recursion on functions. With `A` the adjacency
    operator: `A u₀ = u₁`, `A u₁ = u₂ + 4 u₀`, `A u_{j+2} = u_{j+3} + 3 u_{j+1}` (`Act.aop_uW_zero`,
    `Act.aop_uW_one`, `Act.aop_uW_succ`). If no reduced word of length `1..k+1` leads from `v`
    back to `v` (`Act.uW_eq_zero_of_free`), then
    `K(t)⁻¹ v v ≤ (1 + t^(k+2)/(1 − t)) / (4 − 4 t sVal t)` (`Act.phiJ_eq`, `Act.green_upper`) and
    `tr (K(t)⁻¹ A) ≤ Wsum t + n · 2 t^(k+1)/(1 − t)` (`Act.trace_inv_mul_Amat_le`).
11. **Lower bound for the determinant** (`Sharp`). The inequality of step 3 with the roles
    exchanged (`det_Kmat_step_rev`), steps 9 and 10 and telescoping:
    `Xi z − n · 2 z^(k+1)/(1 − z) ≤ log det K(z)` (`Act.logdet_step_rev`, `Act.logdet_ge`).
12. **From trees to the determinant** (`ForestCount`, `MatrixTree2`). For a connected multigraph
    with `(1 − z) d_v ≤ y²`, `0 ≤ y ≤ 1`: `det K(z) ≤ numTrees · (1 + y)^(n+m)`
    (`det_Kmat_le_numTrees`). Ingredients: the expansion of the determinant in the diagonal
    entries (`det_redMat_expand`), the other half of step 5 (`numForests_ge`,
    `det_redMat_eq_zero`, `det_le_numForests`), the counting lemma `|V| ≤ |R| + |F|`
    (`card_le_of_conn`) and the extension of a rooted forest to a spanning tree
    (`exists_tree_of_forest`, `numForests_le_numTrees_mul`).
13. **Conclusion** (`Sharp`). With `z = 1 − y²/4`:
    `exp (n · (log (27/8) − 2 z^(k+1)/(1 − z) − 3 log (1 + y))) ≤ numTrees` (`Act.numTrees_ge`).
    Choosing `y` small, then `k` large and `n = 3^(k+1) + 2` gives `kenyon_sharp`; here `m = 2n`.

## Remarks

* Steps 10 to 13 apply to every connected graph of an action of four letters (two permutations
  and their inverses) without closed reduced words of length at most `k + 1`.
* No literature search was made for the ingredients of this route. Step 4 says that the Bethe
  approximation is an upper bound for `det (D − zA)`; statements of this kind are known in
  related settings, and no claim of novelty is made for it or for the other steps.
