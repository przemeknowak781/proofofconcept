6. Limitations
--------------
* Mathlib was used as distributed: its compiled files were downloaded by `lake exe cache get`
  and were not replayed in the kernel here.
* The two root modules `Kenyon` and `TreeEntropy` contain only imports and are not replayed
  (in this environment the replay of `Kenyon` exceeds the memory limit); every module with
  declarations is replayed.
* One environment, one run.  The verification was done by the AI agent that wrote the proofs,
  not by an independent party.
* Lean certifies the theorems for the definitions in the files.  That `numTrees` is the number
  of spanning trees in the sense of the paper is a matter of reading four definitions in
  Kenyon/Forest.lean; Kenyon/Examples.lean checks two small cases.
* The proofs follow the second proof of the paper (Section 10), which differs from the proof of
  its Sections 2 to 7; see README.md, "What is NOT proved here".
