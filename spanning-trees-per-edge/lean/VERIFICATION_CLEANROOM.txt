7. Limitations
--------------
- The run was made on the machine on which the package was developed, by the agent that wrote
  it.  The directory, the Mathlib checkout and the download cache were fresh; the elan and Lean
  toolchain installation was the existing one.  This is a reproduction record, not an
  independent review.
- Mathlib was not rebuilt from source: its compiled files were downloaded from the Mathlib
  cache, as `lake exe cache get` does.  leanchecker replays the 8 modules of this package in
  the kernel; it does not replay Mathlib.
- The finite tables are decided by kernel evaluation of natural-number arithmetic
  (`decide +kernel`); this relies on the kernel's built-in arithmetic for natural numbers.
- The check says nothing about the parts of the paper that are not formalised; they are listed
  in README.md under "What is NOT covered".
